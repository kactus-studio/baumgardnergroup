About

Our Legacy

A Family Tradition of Construction Excellence

The Baumgardner family and our dedicated team have been an integral part of South Jersey’s commercial landscape since the late 1970s.

  • Regional Expertise

    Serving the entire state of New Jersey and the Greater Philadelphia region with precision and commitment.

  • Our Core Motto

    "You can never go wrong doing the right thing." This principle guides every project, large or small.

As a family-owned and operated company, we foster long-lasting relationships by treating our clients and team like family. This collaborative, team-first approach ensures that we deliver high-quality results while helping to grow our local economies for years to come.
